Two-stage rocket: booster and motor integration

Dalhousie RocketryLead Propulsion EngineerSept 2026 – present
Problem
Take the team from a 12,000 ft single-stage flight to a two-stage vehicle reaching 35,000 ft at Mach 1.7.
Constraints
  • Pro75 and Pro54 motors held secure through boost, staging and recovery
  • Mach 1.7 loads
  • Fit within the shared airframe
Solution
  • Booster stage designed in Onshape
  • Bulkheads, motor mounts and motor support structure
  • Motor handling and assembly procedures
Result
In the initial design proposal phase, preparing for the Launch Canada critical design review. The bulkhead design and motor support structure are being modelled in Onshape around the Pro75 and Pro54, with flight results to follow against the 35,000 ft, Mach 1.7 target.
